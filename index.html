In Google My Maps, drop a pin on each green, or use “Draw a line › Add line or shape” to trace round it, and name it with the hole number (for example “7”). Add a line from the fairway into each green named “LoP 7” and so on to set the direction of play. Then choose ⋮ › Export to KML/KMZ, tick “Export to a .KML file”, and import the file here. Your pins appear on the course map as yellow flags.

Elevation: Welsh Government LiDAR 2020–22, 1 m terrain model flown December 2020, via DataMapWales, smoothed over about 1 m. Google satellite imagery sits about 1.5 m north of the LiDAR here, so pins and shapes imported from Google My Maps are moved 1.5 m south to line up. Course outline and bunkers © OpenStreetMap contributors. Small breaks under a metre won’t show, and a green rebuilt since 2020 won’t match. Greens were found automatically as flat raised pads; hole numbers are yours to set.
