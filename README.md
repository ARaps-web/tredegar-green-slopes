# Tredegar Park green slopes

Slope maps and putt reads for the greens at Tredegar Park Golf Club, built from Welsh Government 1 m LiDAR (2020–22).

- `index.html`: the app. Tap a green for slope colours, downhill arrows and 5 cm contours, or tap your ball and the hole for a putt read.
- `course.json`: the course elevation model (smoothed 1 m DTM), detected flat pads, bunkers and the course outline.
- `labels.json`: green outlines, hole numbers and lines of play.
- `greenbook.pdf`: the printable green book.

The Google satellite layer uses the Map Tiles API and needs your own key. Use the **Google key** button to paste it in. The key stays in your browser and is never stored in this repo. Restrict it to this site's address and to the Map Tiles API.

Data: Welsh Government LiDAR via DataMapWales. Course outline and bunkers © OpenStreetMap contributors. Satellite imagery © Google.
